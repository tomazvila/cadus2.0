the units is `docs/plans/FRAMEWORK.md`. The handover is
`docs/plans/FRAMEWORK-HANDOVER.md`; its section 8 is the integrated closure audit

Column rules:

- **Implementation** — the commit(s) on `framework/main` that land the
  code. Commits before `699fa89` are the six units of the first swarm, merged at
  `4f6ddb1`. `—` means no commit.
- **Tests** — the test file that holds the evidence, with the test name where one
  test carries the row.
- **Content evidence** — the curriculum, report, or deployment artifact that shows
  the outcome on real content.
- **Residual limitation** — what the row does NOT establish after the work lands.
- **Status** — `done` when every other column is filled and the whole claim of the
  row holds on this branch. `open` when something the row needs is absent: content
  approval, the full gate, a browser run, staging, deployment, or code.
